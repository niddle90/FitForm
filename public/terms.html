What FitForm does

FitForm is a free, client-side toolkit for working with images and PDFs, including:

  • Resizing, cropping, compressing, and converting photos and PDF files, performed locally in your browser.
  • An optional "Vault" feature that lets you save and manage documents in a folder inside your own Google Drive, which the Service connects to only after you sign in with Google and grant permission.

The Service is provided as a static, client-side web application. There is no backend server that receives, stores, or processes your files. All image and PDF processing happens locally on your device, using your browser's own resources, including the WebAssembly modules loaded as part of the app.

Google Drive integration

If you choose to connect Google Drive, FitForm requests the narrowest access scope Google Drive offers for third-party apps (drive.file), which limits FitForm to:

  • files and folders FitForm itself creates in your Drive (e.g. its "FitForm Vault" folder), and
  • files you explicitly pick through Google's own file picker.

FitForm cannot see, list, or modify any other file in your Google Drive. This access is enforced by Google, not merely by how FitForm is written. You can revoke FitForm's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page, which immediately and completely cuts off the Service's access to your Drive.
