FitForm

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

The short version FitForm is a static, client-side app. Your photos and PDFs are resized, compressed, and converted locally in your browser — they are never uploaded to any server we operate. If you connect Google Drive, FitForm talks directly to Google's API from your browser using a short-lived, in-memory access token; we do not receive, store, or have access to your Drive files or their contents.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how FitForm ("the Service", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. FitForm is an independent, student-run project operated by Somnath Pratihar, based in India. For privacy questions, contact somnathpratihar363@gmail.com.

2. Data we do not collect

FitForm has no backend server. As a direct result:

  • We never receive, see, or store the photos, PDFs, or other files you process with the Service.
  • We do not run analytics or tracking scripts that profile you across sites, and we do not sell or share personal data with advertisers.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or other account details for our own purposes — the only account we interact with is your Google account, and only for the specific Drive actions described below.

3. Local, on-device processing

Core features — resizing, cropping, compressing, and converting images and PDFs — run entirely inside your browser using WebAssembly, and process files that stay on your own device. These files never leave your computer as part of that processing.

4. Google Drive integration ("Vault")

The Vault feature is optional. If you turn it on, here is exactly what happens and what we can and cannot see:

4.1 Sign-in

FitForm uses Google Identity Services (Google's own sign-in library) to request an OAuth access token directly from Google, from inside your browser. There is no server-side component to this sign-in — no client secret exists, and no authentication data passes through infrastructure we control.

4.2 Scope of access

FitForm requests only the drive.file scope — the narrowest scope Google Drive offers. This scope limits FitForm to:

  • files and folders that FitForm itself creates (e.g. a "FitForm Vault" folder it creates on first use), and
  • files you explicitly select via Google's own file picker.

FitForm cannot see, list, read, or modify any other file in your Google Drive. This restriction is enforced by Google's servers, independent of FitForm's own code.

4.3 What is stored, and where

DataWhere it livesRetention
Google OAuth access token In memory, in your browser tab only Never written to disk or localStorage; discarded when you close or reload the tab, and expires on its own within about an hour
ID of your "FitForm Vault" folder Your browser's localStorage, on your device Kept until you clear your browser data; used only so FitForm can find the same folder again next time
Your documents (photos, PDFs, etc.) Your own Google Drive, via Google's servers Controlled entirely by you, through Drive itself

We (the developers of FitForm) do not operate any server that stores, logs, or has access to this token, this folder ID, or the contents of your Drive files. Every request to Drive is made directly from your browser to Google's API.

4.4 Revoking access

You can disconnect FitForm from your Google account at any time from the Google Account permissions page, or by signing out inside the app, which revokes the current token with Google. Revoking access immediately stops any further use of your Drive data by FitForm.

5. Cookies and local storage

FitForm does not use tracking cookies. It uses your browser's localStorage only for on-device app preferences (such as light/dark theme, your own Google OAuth Client ID if you're self-hosting the app, and the cached Vault folder ID described above) — never to identify or track you across other sites.

6. Third parties

The only third party involved in the Service is Google, via the Google Identity Services and Google Drive APIs described above, when you choose to use the Vault feature. Your use of Google Drive through FitForm is also subject to Google's own Privacy Policy. FitForm's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
